## 1. Correction to the brief: the official domain

- `https://www.revoo.com/` **does not resolve** (DNS failure). It is not REVOO's domain.
- **The official global site is `https://www.revoo-ev.com/`** (title: "REVOO Global – Electric Bikes & Scooters"). [INT]
- Country sites follow the pattern `revoo-ev.com.<cc>` — confirmed live: Pakistan (`.com.pk`), Bangladesh (`.com.bd`), Nigeria (`.com.ng`), Senegal (`.com.sn`). **No `revoo-ev` site for Rwanda exists** (`.rw`, `.com.rw`, `.co.rw` all fail DNS). [INT, verified by DNS probe]
- REVOO Rwanda's only web link is `hallo-life.com` (Instagram bio and a map listing). That domain currently **does not resolve / returns a server error**. [RW + probe]

**Pitch point:** the Rwanda business has no working official website, and no Rwanda-specific page on the global site.

## 2. Company overview

### 2.1 What REVOO says about itself — official global site [INT]

Source: `revoo-ev.com/about.html`

- Headline: *"Smart, Localized Electric Mobility for Real Everyday Roads."*
- "REVOO is part of **Transsion Holdings'** mobility ecosystem, growing alongside brands with deep experience in global emerging markets."
- **Founded 2023**; expanded to "**more than 10 countries**."
- Mission pillars:
  1. **Together We Can**: work with riders, dealers, local partners to make electric mobility easier to adopt and maintain.
  2. **Think Globally, Act Locally**: global product standards adapted to regional roads and usage.
  3. **Green-Oriented Business**: scale cleaner transportation through practical products and long-term community value.
- Timeline (official): 2022 project launch → 2023 durability testing (**12,000+ km tested in East Africa**) → 2024 "2.0 heavy-duty" models, 3 climate-specific variants → 2025 experience network, 6 cities → 2026 global expansion, 10+ countries.
- Footer tagline: *"Experience the next generation of electric rides. Smart, sustainable, and built for the modern commute."*
- Regions listed on the global map: Latin America (regional network), **East Africa (testing region)**, Pakistan (country focus), South Asia (regional growth).

## 4. Product catalogue (official international) [INT]

**Important framing:** REVOO's range is **step-through electric scooters / light e-mopeds**, not sport motorcycles. The brand and the Rwandan Instagram bio call itself an "electric motorcycle brand", and the brief does too, but the visual product is urban two-wheelers. The creative direction must lean into *urban mobility engineering*, not superbike drama.

**Series structure** (global site): **Y** (entry / compact), **A** (urban scooters), **C** (higher-power commuters), **E** (flagship), **B** (utility / cargo). Models listed on the global site: Y04, Y06, A09, A10, A11, A11-L, A12, A12-L, C32Y, C35Y, E52, B12, B12-L (the `-L` suffix = lithium/LFP variant).

> **Rwanda availability of every model below is [?].** The only Rwanda evidence is "three models, RWF 990k–1.3M" (2023). Do not present any model as locally available until REVOO Rwanda supplies its current list.

**Specification variance warning:** REVOO publishes *different numbers for the same model in different countries* (for example E52 range: 70–110 km Pakistan · 120 km Nigeria · 80–120 km Senegal; E52 top speed 40–75 / 80 / 70–80 km/h). Ranges are test-condition estimates and depend on battery variant and market. **The site must show the numbers as "international specification" and carry a "confirm Rwanda variant" flag until supplied.**

## 5. Battery, charging and technology [INT]

- **Battery chemistries:** graphene battery (lower cost, 6–10 h charge) and lithium / LFP (3–3.5 h standard; B12 fast charge 1.5 h to 80%). LFP warranty is longer (36 mo vs 24 mo) in Pakistan.
- **Charging:** standard household socket; official Rwanda press says "like a smartphone" and mentions a **portable charger** [RW 2023]. No battery-swap network is mentioned anywhere for REVOO.
- **Powertrain:** hub motor (rear wheel; visible in official renders), vector controller (6/12-tube), disc/drum brakes, hydraulic suspension, vacuum (tubeless) tyres.
- **Safety/convenience features named in specs:** CBS combined braking (E52, B12), SOC % monitor (A12), seat storage (B12 33 L), rear-box version of A12 (Pakistan).
- **Not documented anywhere official:** battery cell supplier, IP rating, regenerative braking, app/connectivity, GPS, battery location in frame, frame material. **Do not claim them.** The engineering section must stay generic, or label items "illustrative".

## 7. Target audience

Evidence-based (AfEMA, IGIHE, product positioning):
1. **Personal commuters in Kigali**, especially people spending on moto-taxi rides (REVOO's own sales manager frames savings against taxi fares: "up to RWF 200,000 per month").
2. **Students / young professionals** (A/Y-series, low power, easy to ride; official lifestyle copy: "Campus Commute").
3. **Women and families** (official Pakistan copy: "Perfect for families, easy for women"; step-through frame). Needs sensitivity: validate with REVOO Rwanda before using this angle.
4. **Small business / delivery** (B12 "Built to Serve", rear box options).

Primary acquisition channel: **Instagram → mobile**. Mobile-first design is non-negotiable.
